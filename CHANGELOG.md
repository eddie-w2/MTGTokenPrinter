# Token Printer: what's new

Each "## version" section becomes the release notes shown in the app's update pop-up. Keep it short and plain.
Newest version first.

## 0.9.10
- Shorter QR code sticker: removed the extra rules text, the flavor text and the small print.

## 0.9.09
- The QR code sticker now looks like a Magic card: a framed "MTG Token Printer" card with a {0} mana cost, the QR code as its art, a type line, rules and flavor text, the link and a 1/1 box.
- The QR code and the shared link now go to the download (releases) page.

## 0.9.08
- New share button at the top of Settings: share the GitHub link with any app, or print a QR code sticker that links to it.
- Redesigned long-press buttons: tiles with the icon over the label that slide out from the token and morph as you hover, with text that always fits.
- New FAQ answers: recommended printers, and Google Play / App Store plans.

## 0.9.07
- New print queue: press and hold a token and slide to Add to queue. Each token keeps the settings it was added with, so you can change settings between tokens and print them all at once. Find it at the top of the Decks tab.
- Change printer type: once connected, the Printer window and Settings have a button to try your printer as another type (or detect it automatically again). In the scan list, tap Type next to a printer.
- D20 printers (Nada Print app) are recognised automatically and marked Tested.

## 0.9.06
- Search puts the best matches first: exact names, then names containing the whole word (like "Rat Rogue"), then tokens of that creature type, and partial matches (like "Pirate") last.
- Fixed the extra gap above the last line of rules text on prints.
- New option: Settings > Caption text > Power/toughness in the bottom right.

## 0.9.05
- Fixed: tokens with the same name, type and power/toughness but different abilities were merged into one, so they shared art and abilities (for example every black 1/1 Rat showed Deathtouch). They are now separate tokens.
- Tokens without keyword abilities show the start of their rules text in the list, so look-alikes are easy to tell apart.
- Your favorites, decks and custom art move over to the right token automatically.

## 0.9.04
- Report a bug now opens an email with a short form to fill in, plus your app version, phone and printer details already added.
- The Legal section in Settings folds away; tap its title to open it.
- The version line at the bottom of Settings is centred and shows the copyright and licence.

## 0.9.03
- The FAQ has a search box. Matching answers open by themselves.
- New FAQ answer: how you can support the project.
- The tip links at the bottom of Settings now use the Venmo, Bitcoin and Dogecoin logos.

## 0.9.02
- Prints and exports show a small artist credit at the bottom of the art. You can turn it off in Settings > Caption text > Artist credit.
- An optional tip jar at the bottom of Settings (Venmo, Bitcoin, Dogecoin).
- Tidier requests to Scryfall: the app now names its version, and "Sync now" waits a little between syncs.

## 0.9.01
- Token Printer is now open source, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Settings > Legal has a link to the source code and the license text.
- MX05, MX06, MX08, MX09 and MX10 printers feed paper after a print with blank lines instead of a feed command those models handle badly.

## 0.9.00
- Many more printers: Token Printer now recognises about 80 models that use the Funny Print, Tiny Print / iPrint, Fun Print / iBleem and Phomemo apps (GB01, X6, MX06, MXW01, YT01, XenGro AH-M2, Phomemo M02 and T02, and more), plus basic 58 mm and 80 mm receipt printers.
- The printer list is grouped by the app each printer came with. Printers other than the LX-D02 are marked Experimental until someone confirms they work, so please report how yours prints.
- Printer not listed? Show other Bluetooth devices, then use Try as… to pick the app it came with.
- Prints and exports now fit the connected printer's paper width (including 576-dot printers).
- Icons on the Contact me and Report a bug buttons.

## 0.8.01
- New Contact me and Report a bug buttons in Settings > Help. The bug report email fills in your version, the date and your phone for you.
- A clearer FAQ and welcome guide.
- This version is also a test of the automatic updater.

## 0.8.00
- Token Printer can now update itself. The first time you open it each day it checks for a new version, and Settings > App updates lets you check by hand or install an update you put off.
- Dark mode switch in Settings > Appearance.
- Help: a FAQ in Settings and a welcome guide the first time you open the app.
- Line spacing in the print screen is now even and goes much tighter (down to 0.4), with a Reset button.
- The version number in Settings now always matches the installed version.

## 0.7.04
- Dark mode switch, FAQ and welcome guide, tighter and more even line spacing, correct version number in Settings.
